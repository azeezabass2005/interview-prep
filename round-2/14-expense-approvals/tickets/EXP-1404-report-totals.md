# EXP-1404: Monthly report totals don't add up

**Type:** Bug · **Reporter:** FP&A · **Priority:** Medium

## Description
The March report for the London team shows $12,400 of expenses, but the line items convert to about $14,900 in USD. Most London expenses
are in GBP and EUR.

## Acceptance criteria
- Report totals are in USD, converting each expense at its expense-date rate.

## Out of scope
- Showing totals in other currencies.
