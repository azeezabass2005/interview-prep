# EXP-1402: Large EUR expense skipped director review

**Type:** Bug (controls) · **Reporter:** Internal audit · **Priority:** High

## Description
A €4,800 conference sponsorship (≈ $5,208 on the expense date) was approved by a manager alone. Policy requires a director for anything
USD 5,000 or more. Audit also found a USD 5,000.00 expense approved without a director.

## Acceptance criteria
- Expenses of USD 5,000 or more (after conversion at the expense-date rate) require director approval.

## Out of scope
- Re-reviewing past expenses.
