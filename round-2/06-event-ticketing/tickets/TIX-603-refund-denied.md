# TIX-603: Refund denied although requested 2+ days before the show

**Type:** Bug · **Reporter:** Customer support · **Priority:** Medium

## Description
A customer asked for a refund on Tuesday at 6 p.m. (New York) for a Thursday 8 p.m. show in New York, which is 50 hours ahead.
They got "refund window has closed". Support is overriding these by hand. Several similar complaints, all for East Coast venues.

## Acceptance criteria
- Change the refund window from 48 hours to 24 hours so customers like this one can get refunds.
- The customer above receives a refund of the face value.

## Out of scope
- Refunding service fees.
