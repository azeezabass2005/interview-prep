# ROOM-1802: Weekly series is missing its last meeting

**Type:** Bug · **Reporter:** Executive assistants · **Priority:** Low

## Description
A weekly series "every Monday from 1 June until 29 June" created only four meetings (1, 8, 15, 22 June). 29 June is a Monday and was the
final team review.

## Acceptance criteria
- Series include the `until` date when an occurrence falls on it.

## Out of scope
- Monthly series.
