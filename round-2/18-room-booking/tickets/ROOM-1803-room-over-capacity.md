# ROOM-1803: Rooms booked over capacity

**Type:** Bug · **Reporter:** Health & safety · **Priority:** High

## Description
"Shibuya 2" (capacity 6) was booked for 7 people: an organizer plus 6 invitees. Fire regulations cap the room at 6.

## Acceptance criteria
- Bookings whose organizer + attendees exceed capacity are rejected.

## Out of scope
- Suggesting a bigger room.
