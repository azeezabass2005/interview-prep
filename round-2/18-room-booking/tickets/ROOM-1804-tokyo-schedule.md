# ROOM-1804: Tokyo room schedule shows the wrong day's meetings

**Type:** Bug · **Reporter:** Tokyo office manager · **Priority:** Medium

## Description
The schedule view for a Tokyo room on 21 May is missing the 08:30 meeting, which shows up on 20 May instead. London is mostly fine,
except late-evening meetings.

## Acceptance criteria
- A room's schedule for a date lists exactly the bookings that start on that date in the room's local time zone.

## Out of scope
- Changing how bookings are stored (UTC).
