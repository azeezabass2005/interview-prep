# LB-1904: Weekly leaderboard includes Sunday from the previous week

**Type:** Bug · **Reporter:** Competition organisers · **Priority:** Low

## Description
Monday's weekly board already shows points scored the day before (Sunday). The weekly prize is Monday–Sunday.

## Acceptance criteria
- A score event on Sunday counts towards the week that **ends** that Sunday.
- The week starting Monday only includes events from that Monday onwards.

## Out of scope
- Time zones other than UTC.
