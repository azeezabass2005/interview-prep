Write the section "## 5. Using the AI assistant and communicating".

1. **How you used the AI assistant**: from screenshots, transcript and notes: what you asked it, whether you checked its claims,
   whether you delegated too much or too little. If it wasn't used, say what that cost.
2. **A prompt playbook for this kind of exercise**: 8–10 prompts in code blocks for each stage (mapping the code, checking a rule
   against the code, writing one test, proposing a minimal fix, reviewing the diff), each with a one-line note on why it's shaped that
   way (single goal, explicit output, a boundary like "don't change code yet").
3. **Thinking out loud**: specific moments where your narration helped or left the interviewer out of the loop (timestamps), and
   example sentences to use: stating a hypothesis, predicting a test result, explaining a fix as issue → line → why.
4. **Clarity and delivery**: pace, filler, structure, unclear passages (including speech-recognition problems and what to do about
   them: mic position, pausing, repeating key terms).
